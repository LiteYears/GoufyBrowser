Browser Guide

Quick tips, privacy tools & navigation features.

🛡️ Built-in AdBlocker Active

Integrated hosts-based ad-blocking powered by the curated StevenBlack list. Blocks tracking scripts, banner ads, and malware domains without extra extensions.

🍪 Cookie Banner Auto-Deny Smart

Automatically dismisses GDPR and cookie consent notices by selecting "Reject All" / "Deny", keeping your screen clean and preventing unwanted tracking cookies.

🔒 Anti-Fingerprinting & WebRTC Privacy

Prevents websites from reading device fingerprints and blocks WebRTC requests that can leak your real local IP address even when using VPNs or proxies.

👆 Gestures & Shortcuts

Swipe Up on Toolbar Hide toolbar / New tab
Swipe Down on Toolbar Show toolbar / Tab preview
Long-Press Tab Icon Open new tab directly
Double-Tap Menu Quick site toggles (JS, Dark, Zoom)

📜 UserScripts Support

Customize websites with custom JavaScript user scripts. Manage scripts from Settings > UserScripts to inject custom CSS or site enhancements.
